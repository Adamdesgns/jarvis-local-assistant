# Inbox Brief

Everything waiting on you, in one pass:

- Overdue tasks first, then what is due today
- Today's scheduled jobs and when they fire
- The latest notes captured to memory

The brief is spoken out loud and appended to today's daily note in
`vault/raw/daily/`, so showing up with answers leaves a paper trail.
